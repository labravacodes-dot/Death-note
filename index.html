PROPERTY OF: SHINIGAMI REALM

TO WHOM FINDS THIS LEDGER PAGE 1

A Message to the Finder

Do not be deceived by its ordinary appearance. You hold in your hands a notebook born from the Shinigami realm—a ledger capable of ending any human life simply by inscribing their name upon these pages.

These instructions were intentionally written in English because it is the most popular and widely understood language in your world. There was no room for misunderstanding. The power inside this note is real, absolute, and immediate.

If you possess the courage—or the malice—turn the page, claim ownership, study the absolute laws, and test it for yourself.

HUMAN WORLD REGISTRATION PAGE 2

Bind Your Name to the Ledger

Inscribe your name below to officially claim ownership of this Death Note. Once bound, you accept all consequences under Shinigami law.

HOW TO USE IT: I PAGE 3

I

  • The human whose name is written in this note shall die.
  • This note will not take effect unless the writer has the person's face in their mind when writing their name. Therefore, people sharing the same name will not be affected.
  • If the cause of death is written within 40 seconds of writing the person's name, it will happen.
  • If no cause of death is specified, the person will simply die of a heart attack.
  • After writing the cause of death, details of the death should be written in the next 6 minutes and 40 seconds.
HOW TO USE IT: II PAGE 4

II

  • This note shall become the property of the human world, once it touches the ground of the human world.
  • The owner of the note can recognize the image and voice of its original owner, a Shinigami.
  • The human who uses this note can neither go to Heaven nor Hell.
  • If the time of death is written within 40 seconds after writing the cause of death as a heart attack, the time of death can be manipulated.
  • The Death Note's power will take effect on any torn piece or scrap of a page.
HOW TO USE IT: III PAGE 5

III

  • As long as the name is written on a page or piece of paper, the instrument used to write does not matter (pen, pencil, blood).
  • A person who gains the Shinigami Eyes can see a human's name and remaining life span just by looking at their face.
  • The price for obtaining the Shinigami Eyes is half of the person's remaining life span.
  • If a Death Note owner accidentally misspells a target's name four times, that target becomes permanently immune to being killed by that note.
  • If a Death Note owner intentionally misspells a name four times to save a target, the owner will instantly die instead.
HOW TO USE IT: IV PAGE 6

IV

  • Suicide is a valid cause of death. Essentially, all humans are thought to possess the possibility to commit suicide.
  • If two or more Death Notes are used to write the same name within 0.06 seconds, the entries cancel each other out and the target will not die.
  • If a target's remaining lifespan is less than 12 minutes, or if they are older than 124 years, the Death Note cannot take their life.
  • Giving up ownership of the Death Note strips away all associated memories of it.
  • Your memories of the Death Note will return only if you physically touch the notebook again.
HOW TO USE IT: V PAGE 7

V

  • A Shinigami can never die, even if stabbed in the heart with a knife or shot through the head with a gun.
  • However, there are ways to kill a Shinigami, which are generally unknown to the Shinigami themselves.
  • The conditions for a Shinigami to extend their life involve writing human names in their own Death Note.
  • If a Shinigami decides to use the Death Note to kill a human out of love or to save them, the Shinigami will disintegrate and die.
  • The human who was saved by this act inherits the remaining lifespan of the deceased Shinigami.
HOW TO USE IT: VI PAGE 8

VI

  • Lending or trading the Death Note between humans is permitted as long as ownership is formally transferred.
  • The number of pages in the Death Note will never run out; they are infinite.
  • If the owner loses the Death Note or it is stolen, ownership is automatically forfeited after 49 days if not recovered.
  • A Shinigami must always accompany the human who possesses their notebook in the human world.
  • All rules established within this ledger constitute the absolute decree of the Shinigami King.
HOW TO USE IT: VII PAGE 9

VII

  • A human cannot use a Death Note if they are less than 6 years old in human chronology.
  • If a notebook is owned by multiple humans simultaneously, any owner can write in it and execute targets.
  • When writing multiple names on the same page, if the causes of death are identical, they execute concurrently.
  • If different causes are written for different names on the same page, execution proceeds in order of writing time.
  • A death condition cannot involve shortening another completely unrelated person's natural lifespan beyond their own.
HOW TO USE IT: VIII PAGE 10

VIII

  • A Shinigami may possess up to two Death Notes at a time in the human world, but must return one upon request.
  • If a human drops their Death Note and loses possession, they lose all memory of it instantly.
  • If another human picks up the dropped notebook, that new human gains full ownership and memories.
  • A Shinigami cannot reveal the true name or lifespan of any human to anyone, including their notebook owner.
  • The bargain for the Shinigami Eyes is permanent and cannot be refunded or undone by the Shinigami King.
HOW TO USE IT: IX PAGE 11

IX

  • Photocopies, digital scans, or digital photographs of Death Note pages hold zero supernatural power.
  • Writing on a digital screen or typed document emulating the notebook will cause no effect whatsoever.
  • If a page is completely filled with writing, the Shinigami can provide additional blank sheets upon request.
  • A human owner may pass their notebook to a subordinate, provided ownership guidelines are strictly observed.
  • Using a pseudonym or fake stage name will not work; the target's true birth name must be inscribed.
HOW TO USE IT: X PAGE 12

X

  • If a person dies due to the Death Note, their death certificate will officially record a natural or accidental cause.
  • No human authority or forensic investigator can trace a Death Note heart attack back to supernatural causes.
  • A Shinigami is forbidden from eating anything other than apples from the human world without facing realm penalties.
  • Apples in the human world provide Shinigami with an addictive, hallucinatory sensation of crisp satisfaction.
  • The realm rules are absolute and binding across both the human world and the dimension of the dead.
HOW TO USE IT: XI PAGE 13

XI

  • The final authority on all disputes regarding Death Note ownership rests solely with the King of the Shinigami.
  • Any attempt to deceive the Shinigami King regarding notebook usage results in immediate execution in the nether realm.
  • Shinigami must keep written records of human deaths to maintain balance in the underworld archives.
  • If a human uses the notebook for financial gain alone, the Shinigami realm applies strict karmic taxes.
  • The possession of multiple notebooks by a single mortal amplifies the psychological burden exponentially.
HOW TO USE IT: XII PAGE 14

XII

  • This ledger, marked as Edition No. 13, represents the complete accumulated codex of human-realm instructions.
  • Study these laws carefully before inscribing any name upon the pages that follow.
  • Once an entry is logged in the human world ledger, it cannot be undone by any mortal hands.
  • Turn the page to review final decrees before execution.
  • May your judgment be swift and absolute.
HOW TO USE IT: XIII PAGE 15

XIII

  • The rules of the Death Note cannot be altered, bypassed, or rewritten by any mortal or Shinigami.
  • Should the notebook ever be destroyed or lost forever, its lingering supernatural signature remains bound to its owner.
  • All instructions are finalized in this codex. Turn the page to begin recording entries in the human world ledger.
  • Proceed with absolute caution. There is no turning back once a name is inscribed.
  • The final page of instructions concludes here.
